      Behind the numbers Sources, formulas & scenario assumptions

      How the income is calculated

      • Down payment = price × down payment % ÷ 100. Base loan = price − down payment.
      • Upfront premium = base loan × upfront % ÷ 100. Financed loan = base loan + premium if financed.
      • Principal and interest use fixed-rate amortization: L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), with r = annual rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12 and n = term years × 12. At 0% interest, payment = L ÷ n.
      • Mortgage insurance = base loan × annual MI % ÷ 100 ÷ 12, unless a fixed monthly override is selected. Default MI: base loan × 0.005 ÷ 12.
      • Monthly housing = principal & interest + annual taxes ÷ 12 + annual hazard insurance ÷ 12 + monthly mortgage insurance + HOA + other monthly housing costs.
      • Front-end required annual income = monthly housing ÷ 0.45 × 12. Back-end required annual income = (monthly housing + all borrower monthly debts) ÷ 0.5699 × 12.
      • Required annual income is the greater of those two values. DTI uses combined gross income before taxes. No income entered means DTI is not available, not 0%.
